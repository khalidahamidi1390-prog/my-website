FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

How does the Profit Calculator work?

Enter your costs and desired profit percentage. Business Toolkit calculates your total cost, profit amount, selling price and margin.

Is Business Toolkit free?

The current demo includes the core tools. A future Free/Pro/Business model can be connected to accounts and payments.

Can I save my invoices?

The dashboard architecture is prepared for saved invoices and history; account storage can be connected later.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. The interface is responsive and adapts its layout for phones, tablets and desktops.

Is my data safe?

The current demo performs calculations locally in your browser. A production account system should add secure authentication and encrypted server-side storage.
